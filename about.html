WHY WE EXIST

Millions around the world never get the opportunity to experience the beauty of our heritage. Barriers of distance, cost, time and mobility should never limit human curiosity.

Heritage & Culture removes those barriers and opens the door to discovery.

OUR MISSION
  • Preserve and document heritage for future generations
  • Make culture and knowledge accessible to all
  • Inspire meaningful travel and responsible tourism
  • Empower local communities and economies
  • Build sustainable technology for a better world
OUR VISION

To become the world's most trusted XR platform for heritage and tourism — where exploration, learning, planning and preservation come together in one immersive ecosystem.

Getting Started

Heritage & Culture lets you explore India's most iconic destinations through fully immersive digital experiences — including 360° panoramic tours, VR 180° stereoscopic videos, and interactive environments — all without physically being there. It's travel reimagined for the digital age.

Unlike traditional travel videos, our tours are fully immersive and interactive. You can look around freely in 360°, navigate through locations at your own pace, and interact with embedded hotspots containing historical context, images, and narrated stories. You're not just watching — you're exploring.

Not at all! Our experiences work beautifully on desktop browsers, mobile phones, and tablets. However, for the most immersive experience, we recommend using a VR headset like Meta Quest. Think of it as the difference between watching a movie on your phone and watching it in a cinema — both are great, but VR takes it to another level.

  • Scenic beaches and backwaters
  • Cultural festivals and living traditions
